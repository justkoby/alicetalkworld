ABOUT ALICE TALK WORLD

We are a movement building a future where young leaders have the knowledge, skills, and networks to succeed and inspire change.

Alice Talk World is a Gen Z and youth-led movement that thrives on leadership, influence, and inclusiveness. We are dedicated to building a future where young leaders are equipped with the knowledge, skills, and networks to succeed. Our commitment is to social impact, empowering students with the skills and confidence to navigate their career paths.

Our Vision

We envision a world where empowered young people are at the forefront of social change, leading with influence, inclusivity, and a deep commitment to their communities. A future where every student, regardless of background, has the confidence and skills to navigate their career paths and make a tangible, positive impact.

Our Mission

Alice Talk World's mission is to ignite the potential of young leaders. We do this by providing transformative skills training, fostering mentorship, and creating platforms for hands-on social impact projects. Through initiatives like our health awareness campaigns and community support programs, we turn passion into action, building a network of changemakers dedicated to a more equitable and inspiring world.

OUR HISTORY

Building on this success, we transitioned to organizing free conferences, workshops, and awareness programs to reach and impact a larger audience. Over the years, our commitment to addressing critical issues facing students has remained unwavering.

Our journey has only just begun, and we remain dedicated to driving meaningful change in the lives of students across the nation. Today, Alice TalkWorld stands as a beacon of innovation, leadership, and inclusiveness, committed to equipping Ghanaian students with the skills and networks needed to succeed academically, professionally, and socially.

OUR THEMATIC AREAS

LEADERSHIP

Cultivating leadership skills and qualities that empower individuals to make a positive impact on their communities and the world.

ENTREPRENEURSHIP

Fostering an entrepreneurial spirit and nurturing the next generation of business leaders and innovators.

CLIMATE CHANGE

Dedicated to combating climate change through sustainable practices and environmental stewardship in the pursuit of a better future.

TECH SPACE

Advancing technological innovation and digital literacy as cornerstones for empowering the next generation of leaders.

OBJECTIVES

Inclusivity

Ensuring equal access to opportunities, representation, and resources for all students.
