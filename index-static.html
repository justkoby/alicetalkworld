OUR VISION

A FUTURE OF
INFLUENCE &
INCLUSIVITY

We are building a future where every young leader is equipped with the knowledge, voice, and network to succeed and inspire the next generation.

Get Involved

In an era characterized by rapid technological advancements and evolving industry demands, the transition from academia to the professional world presents significant challenges for students. While tertiary institutions provide foundational knowledge, there remains a critical gap in equipping students with the practical skills, industry insights, and professional networks necessary for career success.

Alice TalkWorld stands as a strategic initiative designed to bridge this gap. By fostering engagement between students and industry leaders, this platform serves as a conduit for knowledge transfer, professional development, and career advancement. Through mentorship programs, skill-building workshops, and networking opportunities, Alice TalkWorld empowers students to navigate the complexities of the job market with confidence and competence.

Guided by its core values of Leadership, Influence, Innovation, Passion, and Inclusiveness, Alice TalkWorld is committed to cultivating a generation of forward-thinking professionals who will drive innovation and contribute meaningfully to their respective industries. This initiative represents a transformative step toward creating a workforce that is not only academically proficient but also industry-ready.

As we embark on this journey, we invite all stakeholders, students, educators, industry professionals, and policymakers to support and participate in this noble endeavor. By working together, we can shape a future where talent meets opportunity, and where the leaders of tomorrow are equipped to excel in an ever-evolving global landscape.

LATEST NEWS

NOV 01, 2025

Alice Talk World @ 5: Anniversary Conference at the British Council

Alice Talk World celebrated five years of Change, Empowerment and Impact with a milestone anniversary conference at the British Council in Accra. Under the theme “Shaping the Future: Leadership, Innovation and Global Impact,” students, young professionals and partners gathered for a full day of conversations, networking and inspiration.

The event featured keynote sessions, interactive dialogues and live experiences that highlighted how Alice Talk World has supported youth leadership across West Africa since 2020.

NOV 01, 2025

Youth Panel: Shaping the Future of African Leadership

The Youth Panel brought together dynamic young leaders including Dr Ekua Amoako, Alfred Eli Dei, Dr Khadija Owusu, Paa Kwesi Foison and Mariam Majeed, with David Quaye as moderator.

Together, they explored how young Africans can lead with courage, leverage technology, and build careers that create real social impact. From personal stories to practical advice, the conversation left attendees energized and ready to take action.

NOV 01, 2025

High Level Panel: Policy, Innovation & Global Impact

On the high level panel, leaders including Dr Augustine Blay, Prophet Prakash Pyne, Sheena Sue Biney, Nana-Kwame Asafo-Adjei Ayen and Dr Vanessa Aseye Atikpui, moderated by Mrs Belinda Boadu, discussed how policy, innovation and partnerships can unlock opportunities for young people.

The panel highlighted the importance of strategic collaboration between government, private sector and youth-led initiatives to drive sustainable development and inclusive growth across the continent.
